Publish to your social accounts from your AI assistant.

MCP Socials is a self-hosted Model Context Protocol server. It lets an assistant such as Claude publish posts you approve to your own Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, YouTube channel and X account, using each platform's official API.

Facebook PagesText, link, photo and video posts, with scheduling.
InstagramImages, reels, carousels and stories.
YouTubeVideo uploads with titles, tags, privacy and scheduled release.
XPosts and threads with images or video.

How it works

You run MCP Socials on your own computer and sign in to each platform once. Your access tokens stay on that computer. Nothing is posted until you ask your assistant to post, and every post goes to accounts you own or manage.
